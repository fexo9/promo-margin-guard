# Promo Margin Guard — v2.0

**Will your sale actually make money?** A pre-sale calculator for e-commerce sellers (Shopify, Etsy, marketplaces) who run discounts, coupon codes, and seasonal sales.

Enter your price, cost, and planned discount *before* you launch — it tells you whether the promo will make money or quietly lose it, and exactly how many extra units you'd need just to break even.

**The formula:** Extra units needed = Discount% ÷ (Margin% − Discount%). A 10% discount on a 40% margin needs ~33% more units. A 20% discount at the same margin needs 100% more.

## The core formula

**Extra units needed to break even = Discount% ÷ (Margin% − Discount%)**

Margin% = (Price − COGS) ÷ Price, before platform fees, payment fees, shipping, and returns.

## Mini worked example (EXAMPLE / fictional data)

A fictional candle shop sells a candle at **$32**, COGS **$14**.

- Margin = (32 − 14) ÷ 32 = **56.25%**
- Planned discount: **20%**. Extra units needed = 20 ÷ (56.25 − 20) = 20 ÷ 36.25 = **55.17%**
- The shop normally sells 150/month. To break even it must sell 150 × 1.5517 = **233 units**.
- Discounted price = **$25.60**. Profit per unit drops from $18.00 to **$11.60** — before fees.
- After example fees (platform 6.5% + $0.20, payment 2.9% + $0.30, shipping $4.50, return reserve 3%), true profit per unit drops from **$9.03** to **$3.43**.

## Checklist excerpt — before every sale

- [ ] True margin known for THIS product, not the store average.
- [ ] Real fee settings entered (platform, payment, shipping, return reserve). Defaults are examples, not your numbers.
- [ ] Break-even volume computed and written down, not guessed.
- [ ] Discount is below the margin. If discount ≥ margin, stop — every unit loses money.
- [ ] Expected uplift is honest (based on past promos, not hope) and beats break-even.
- [ ] Fixed costs counted (ads, extra staff, packaging).
- [ ] Promo beats doing nothing (incremental profit positive).
